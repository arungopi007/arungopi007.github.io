01 — About

Engineering the bridge between legacy and cloud

I am a Mainframe Application Modernization Architect with more than 20 years across design, development, and large-scale transformation of mainframe applications for enterprise clients in the UK and US. I lead engagements end-to-end — from portfolio analysis and target architecture to phased, risk-free delivery.

My toolkit spans COBOL, JCL, VSAM, CICS, IMS and DB2, plus niche platforms such as Mantis, Supra and ProIV. Beyond the mainframe, I automate with Python, monitor modern estates with Datadog, and land workloads on Azure via Micro Focus rehosting.

I believe modernization is about balance: keep the mainframe where it earns its keep, and move what has outgrown it — rehosting, refactoring, or exposing legacy assets as APIs. Every decision is grounded in data and de-risked by design.
