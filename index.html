About Me

I am a Mainframe Application Modernization Architect with more than 20 years of expertise in mainframe application development, maintenance, and modernization. I lead large-scale engagements, delivering innovative solutions that enable phased and risk-free implementations for enterprise clients.

My core competencies span COBOL, JCL, VSAM, CICS, IMS, and DB2, complemented by deep knowledge of niche mainframe technologies such as Mantis, Supra, and ProIV. Working directly with clients across the UK and the US, I have automated tedious tasks with Python and architected major modernization and rehosting programs.

I am passionate about finding the right balance between keeping mainframes where they add the most value and modernizing workloads that have outgrown them — whether that means rehosting to the cloud, refactoring, or exposing legacy assets as APIs.
